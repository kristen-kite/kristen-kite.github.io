Kristen Kite is a design educator and expert in UX research and design.

She believes projects - whether it’s designing an enterprise app, conducting a field study, or teaching a class - are most successful when collaborators feel safe taking creative risks and learning together.

When she’s outside the design studio, Kristen can be found dancing in her kitchen, sewing her own wardrobe, or enjoying a Manhattan on her porch with her partner.
